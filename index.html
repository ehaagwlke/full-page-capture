Three steps.

The page scrolls by itself while the extension works.

1

Start

Click the toolbar icon and choose Capture Full Page, right-click the page, or press Alt Shift P.

2

Wait a moment

Watch the percentage in the popup, on the badge or in the tab title.

3

Edit and save

A preview tab opens with your image. Annotate it if you like, then save the PNG.

Your pages stay yours.

Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • No account, no server, no analytics and no tracking. The extension makes no network requests of its own.
  • Captures are built in your browser and deleted when you close the preview tab, or at the latest when the browser next starts.
  • The extension only works on the tab you start it on.

Good to know
  • Browser pages (chrome://) and the Chrome Web Store cannot be captured. Chrome does not allow it.
  • To capture local files, turn on Allow access to file URLs for the extension.
  • A capture stops at 30,000 CSS pixels in height or after 60 seconds, whichever comes first, and tells you so.
  • Very animated pages, and sites that reveal content only while you scroll with a mouse, can differ from the live page.
