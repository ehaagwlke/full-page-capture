Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short

Full Page Capture does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any data. Everything happens on your computer, in your browser.

What the extension reads

When you start a capture, the extension reads the visible content and the layout of the one tab you started it on (the page text, images and styles as they appear), only to photograph that page. It also reads the page title, to name the file you save.

Where it goes

The screenshots are joined into one image inside your browser. The image is kept in the browser's local extension storage and is deleted when you close the preview tab, or at the latest when the browser next starts. The image reaches your disk only when you click Save.

What it does not do

It makes no network requests of its own. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no remote code. It does not read the content of any tab other than the one you start it on. To switch its icon off on pages that cannot be captured (browser pages, the Chrome Web Store), it checks the address of open tabs on your device. Those addresses are not stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

The permissions the extension asks for, and why, are listed on the home page under Privacy. Each one is used only for the capture, the preview and the save.

Children

The extension collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page and the date above changes.

Contact

Use the support contact shown on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
